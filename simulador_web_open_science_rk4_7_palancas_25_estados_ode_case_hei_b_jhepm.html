2. Tri-Pillar Functional Orthogonality (Sobol Policy-Space Decomposition, N = 1,280)

Each policy family controls a distinct institutional dimension; isolated reforms leave the other two dimensions exposed.

Pillar 1: Governance (DORA + Prom. + Pay)

Explains 100.0% of faculty stress variance, slashes APC spending by 44.6% (1.32 Bn COP/yr saved), and expands Full Professors +62.7%—yet leaves Master's enrollment flat (17.1).

Pillar 2: Market (Marginal Tuition + Alumni)

Explains 98.7% of Master's cohort variance and 43.5% of reputation—yet preserves faculty burnout above the 55-pt cliff (56.29 pts) if deployed alone.

Pillar 3: AI Pedagogy (Brain-First + Dual-Clock)

Explains 56.7% of external reputation variance by neutralizing generative AI cognitive offloading and lifting the human employability premium from 0.703 to 1.250.
